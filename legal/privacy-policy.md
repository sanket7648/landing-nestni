## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how we (“Nestni”, “we”, “us” or “our”) collect, receive, use, store, share and otherwise process personal data when you access or use the platform, web application, mobile application, software, features and related services (collectively, the “Platform” or “Services”).

Nestni helps property owners and authorised property managers manage property information, buildings, floors, rooms, tenant records, identity and tenancy documents, rent schedules, pending rent status, vacant rooms and historical tenant records.

This Policy applies to property owners, landlords, tenants, authorised property managers, staff members, invited users, visitors and anyone whose personal data is uploaded to or processed through Nestni.

Please read this Policy together with the Terms of Use. If you do not agree with this Policy, do not create an account or use the Platform. Where applicable law requires consent for a particular processing activity, we will seek that consent separately or through an appropriate in-product notice.

## 2. Important definitions

- “Personal Data” means information about an identified or identifiable individual, as defined under applicable data protection law.
- “Owner” means a person or entity that owns, leases, controls or is otherwise authorised to manage a property recorded in Nestni.
- “Tenant” means an individual occupying, applying to occupy or previously having occupied a room, unit or property managed through Nestni.
- “Property Manager” means a person authorised by an Owner to manage some or all property records or operations.
- “Tenant Records” means information relating to a current or former tenant, including contact details, occupancy dates, rent records, uploaded documents and tenancy history.
- “Documents” means identity proofs, photographs, rental or lease agreements, address proofs, receipts, notices and other files uploaded to the Platform.
- “Service Providers” means third parties engaged to host, secure, maintain, support or provide functionality for the Platform.

## 3. Personal data we collect

The categories of data we collect depend on the role you have, the features you use and the information an Owner or Property Manager enters about you.

### 3.1 Information you provide directly

- Account and contact information: name, mobile number, email address, username, password credentials or authentication identifiers, profile photograph and communication preferences.
- Owner and manager information: business or property-management name, contact details, role, account permissions and information reasonably needed to establish authority to manage a property.
- Property information: property name, address, locality, building details, number of floors, floor identifiers, room or unit numbers, room type, occupancy status, amenities, rent amount, deposit amount and property photographs.
- Tenant information: name, contact number, email address, current or previous room/unit, move-in and move-out dates, emergency contact details where supplied, tenancy status and notes entered by an authorised user.
- Identity and tenancy documents: identity documents or numbers, address proofs, photographs, rental agreements, signatures, notices, receipts and other documents uploaded by you or by an Owner/Property Manager.
- Rent and ledger information: rent due dates, rent amount, payment status, outstanding balance, late-payment notes, payment dates, receipts and transaction references entered or received through an enabled integration.
- Support and communication information: messages, support requests, feedback, complaint details and records of communications with Nestni.

### 3.2 Information collected automatically

When you access the Platform, we may collect technical and usage information such as browser and device type, operating system, app version, log data, pages or screens viewed, features used, timestamps, crash reports, approximate location derived from IP address, session identifiers and security events. We may use cookies, local storage or similar technologies where the Platform supports them.

### 3.3 Information received from other sources

We may receive information about you from an Owner, Property Manager or another user who is authorised to manage the relevant property; from authentication, hosting, analytics, messaging or other service providers; or from a third-party verification or payment provider if Nestni integrates such a service and you use it. The person uploading another individual's data must have the authority and lawful basis required to do so.

## 4. Why we use personal data

We use personal data for purposes reasonably connected with providing, securing and improving Nestni, including:

- creating, authenticating and administering user accounts;
- allowing Owners and authorised Property Managers to add and manage properties, buildings, floors, rooms and units;
- maintaining occupancy information and identifying rooms marked vacant, occupied, reserved or under maintenance;
- creating and maintaining tenant profiles and current or historical tenancy records;
- storing, organising, retrieving and sharing tenancy or identity documents with users who have the required permissions;
- recording rent schedules, due dates, pending amounts, payment status and receipts, and generating reminders or reports;
- sending service messages, account alerts, rent reminders and security notifications;
- responding to support requests and resolving technical or account issues;
- maintaining backups, troubleshooting, auditing access, detecting fraud or misuse and protecting the Platform;
- understanding Platform usage and improving reliability, usability and features;
- complying with applicable laws, lawful requests and legal obligations, and establishing, exercising or defending legal claims.

We will not use tenant identity documents or tenancy records for unrelated advertising or marketing purposes. We will not sell personal data. If we propose a new purpose that is not compatible with the purpose for which the data was collected, we will provide any notice and obtain any consent required by applicable law.

## 5. Role-based access and sharing between users

Nestni is designed to support property administration. Depending on the permissions configured for an account, an Owner may be able to view records relating to the Owner’s properties, including tenant profiles, occupancy, rent status and uploaded documents. A Property Manager may receive access only to the properties and functions assigned by the Owner. A Tenant may be able to view or update only the information and documents made available to that Tenant through the Platform.

Owners and Property Managers are responsible for assigning access only to people who need it, reviewing permissions when roles change, and removing access promptly when a person no longer requires it. Users should not assume that every item in a property record is visible to every other user. The actual access available is determined by the Platform's permissions and the configuration in effect.

Nestni does not authorise an Owner or Property Manager to use a tenant's personal data for unrelated purposes, harassment, discrimination, public disclosure or any purpose prohibited by law.

## 6. When we share personal data

We may share personal data only as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, subject to applicable law:

- With authorised users: Owners, Property Managers, staff and tenants, but only to the extent permitted by the relevant property relationship and access settings.
- With service providers: cloud hosting, database, file storage, backup, authentication, email, SMS, WhatsApp or push-notification providers, customer support, security, analytics and software maintenance providers acting on our instructions or otherwise under their own applicable terms.
- With professional advisers: lawyers, auditors, accountants, insurers and consultants where reasonably necessary and subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations.
- For legal and safety reasons: courts, regulators, government authorities, law enforcement agencies or other parties where disclosure is required by law, legal process or a valid request, or is reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, security or prevent fraud.
- Business transfers: a successor, investor or acquiring entity in connection with a merger, restructuring, financing, sale or transfer of all or part of our business, subject to appropriate safeguards and notices where required.
- At your direction: another person or service where you ask us to share information or clearly enable an integration.

We do not permit service providers to use personal data entrusted to them by Nestni for their own independent purposes unless that use is separately disclosed and permitted by law. We do not share tenant documents with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

## 7. Identity documents and verification

Nestni may provide tools to upload, store, organise or review identity and tenancy documents. Unless a specific verification feature is expressly described in the Platform, uploading a document does not mean Nestni has independently authenticated it, checked it against a government database, confirmed the identity of its holder, or certified its legal validity.

If Nestni introduces a third-party identity-verification service, we will provide additional information about the provider, the data shared, the purpose, the applicable retention arrangements and any consent or authorisation required before the feature is used.

Owners and Property Managers must collect and upload identity documents only where they have a lawful basis and appropriate authority. They should avoid collecting more information than necessary and should not upload documents belonging to a person who has not been appropriately informed or whose data they are not authorised to provide.

## 8. Payments and financial information

Nestni records rent amounts, due dates and payment status but does not itself collect, hold, transfer or settle rent. If you use a third-party payment service outside Nestni, that service processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy. Nestni does not collect or store full card credentials, banking passwords, UPI PINs or other authentication secrets.

## 9. Cookies, analytics and similar technologies

We may use essential cookies or similar technologies to maintain sessions, remember preferences, protect accounts and keep the Platform functioning. If we use analytics or non-essential tracking technologies, we will provide appropriate information and controls where required by law. You can manage cookies through your browser settings, although disabling essential cookies may affect Platform functionality.

## 10. Data storage, security and transfers

We use reasonable technical, organisational and administrative safeguards designed to protect personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, loss or destruction. Depending on the Platform architecture, safeguards may include access controls, role-based permissions, encryption in transit, secure storage, authentication controls, logging, backups, vulnerability management and restricted staff access.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. Users must protect their login credentials, use secure devices, avoid sharing accounts and notify us promptly if they suspect unauthorised access. We will handle a personal data breach in accordance with applicable law and our incident-response procedures, including notifications where required.

Personal data may be stored or processed in India or in another country where our hosting or service providers operate, subject to applicable legal requirements and appropriate safeguards.

## 11. Data retention and deletion

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, to provide the Services, to maintain legitimate property and tenancy records requested by the relevant Owner, and to comply with applicable legal, accounting, tax, security and dispute-resolution requirements.

Because Nestni supports historical tenant records, closing a tenancy or marking a room vacant will not automatically erase the associated tenant record. The Owner may retain relevant tenancy history in accordance with applicable law and its own lawful recordkeeping needs. Nestni will apply the retention and deletion settings and instructions available to it, subject to legal obligations and legitimate retention requirements.

When an account is closed or a deletion request is received, we will assess the request and delete, anonymise or restrict personal data where required and legally permitted. Some information may remain in backups for a limited period or be retained where necessary to comply with law, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, enforce agreements or establish or defend legal claims. We will not retain data indefinitely merely because it may be useful.

## 12. Your privacy rights and choices

Subject to applicable law and the circumstances of the processing, you may have the right to:

- request information about personal data processed by Nestni;
- request access to, correction or updating of inaccurate or incomplete personal data;
- request erasure or deletion of personal data where applicable;
- withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
- raise a grievance or complaint about our handling of personal data;
- nominate another individual or exercise any other right available under applicable law.

To make a request, email info@nestni.com. We may need to verify your identity and clarify whether you are requesting data held by Nestni or records controlled by an Owner or Property Manager. If a request concerns tenant information uploaded and controlled by an Owner, Nestni may need to refer the request to that Owner or coordinate with the Owner, depending on the applicable legal roles and circumstances.

Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already carried out lawfully before withdrawal. It may also mean that certain features cannot be provided where the relevant data is essential to the Service or where another lawful basis applies.
